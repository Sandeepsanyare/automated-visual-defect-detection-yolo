\chapter{Conclusion and Future Research}
\label{chap:conclusion}

This thesis has detailed the development and rigorous optimization of a surface defect detection system based on the YOLOv8s architecture. By implementing a modular two-phase training strategy and a custom tiling inference engine, we have achieved a model that satisfies both classification accuracy and localization precision requirements for high-speed industrial manufacturing.

\section{Summary of Contributions}

The primary contributions of this research are summarized as follows:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Tiling Inference Strategy:} Developed and implemented a robust sliding-window tiling method for processing high-resolution industrial imagery. This approach circumvents the limitations of standard image resizing, ensuring zero information loss and preserving pixel-level detail for small defect detection.
    
    \item \textbf{Optimization Pipeline:} Demonstrated a rigorous \textit{Coarse-to-Fine} hyperparameter tuning methodology powered by the \textbf{Optuna} Bayesian Optimization framework. The study proves that structured automated searches significantly outperform manual heuristic-based tuning for complex metallic surface datasets.
    
    \item \textbf{Validated Performance Metrics:} Established an optimized performance baseline for the dataset, achieving a \textbf{mAP@50 of 0.82676} and a \textbf{mAP@50-95 of 0.5296}. These results represent a statistically significant improvement over non-optimized YOLOv8 baselines.
\end{itemize}

\section{Directions for Future Research}

The following avenues are proposed to extend the current research framework:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{YOLOv11 Integration:} Future work should explore the recently released YOLOv11 and its enhanced transformer-based backbones to further increase sensitivity to low-contrast surface defects such as crazing.
    
    \item \textbf{Semi-Supervised Learning:} Given that labeling industrial data is labor-intensive and expensive, implementing a semi-supervised or self-supervised framework—such as using masked autoencoders—could effectively utilize the vast amounts of unlabelled data generated by automated production lines.
    
    \item \textbf{Edge Optimization:} Future deployment strategies should focus on quantizing the model to INT8 or FP16 formats. Utilizing \textbf{TensorRT} for deployment on NVIDIA Jetson or similar low-power edge devices would facilitate real-time, on-site industrial monitoring.
\end{enumerate}

In conclusion, deep learning-based AOI systems are no longer a futuristic laboratory concept but a practical, ready-to-deploy solution for modern quality assurance.
